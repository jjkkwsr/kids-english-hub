Part 2: Speaking Task Reference (朗讀任務 Be 動詞批改參考)

1. The clown /klaʊn/ and the pirate /ˈpaɪrət/ [are] jumping on the farm /fɑrm/.
💡 單字對照: clown = 小丑 | pirate = 海盜 | farm = 農場

2. A tall /tɔl/ farmer /ˈfɑrmɚ/ [is] looking at the kangaroo /ˌkæŋɡəˈru/ and the panda /ˈpændə/.
💡 單字對照: tall = 高大的 | farmer = 農夫 | kangaroo = 袋鼠 | panda = 熊貓

3. The cute puppy /ˈpʌpi/ and the small kitten /ˈkɪtn̩/ [are] playing with a rabbit /ˈræbɪt/.
💡 單字對照: puppy = 小狗 | kitten = 小貓 | rabbit = 兔子

4. Look! A green parrot /ˈpærət/ [is] sitting on the cage /kedʒ/, and a black bat /bæt/ [is] under the roof /ruf/.
💡 單字對照: parrot = 鸚鵡 | cage = 籠子 | bat = 蝙蝠 | roof = 屋頂

5. The smart dolphin /ˈdɑlfɪn/ and the big grey whale /wel/ [are] swimming away from the scary shark /ʃɑrk/.
💡 單字對照: dolphin = 海豚 | whale = 鯨魚 | shark = 鯊魚

6. The funny comic book /ˈkɑmɪk bʊk/ and fast roller skates /ˈrolɚ skets/ [are] on the desk next to a lion /ˈlaɪən/ toy.
💡 單字對照: comic book = 漫畫書 | roller skates = 輪滑鞋 | lion = 獅子
