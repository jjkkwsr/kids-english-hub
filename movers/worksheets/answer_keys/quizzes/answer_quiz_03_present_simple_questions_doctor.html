Part 2: Speaking Task Reference (朗讀任務問句助動詞圈選與 KK 音標參考)

1. [Does] the doctor /ˈdɑktɚ/ with a dark moustache /ˈmʌstæʃ/ check your neck /nɛk/ and back /bæk/?
💡 單字對照: doctor = 醫生 | moustache = 八字鬍 | neck = 脖子 | back = 背部

2. [Does] the kind nurse /nɝs/ help the patient with a bad headache /ˈhɛdˌek/ and a high temperature /ˈtɛmpɚətʃɚ/?
💡 單字對照: nurse = 護理師 | headache = 頭痛 | temperature = 體溫/發燒

3. [Do] you have a severe stomachache /ˈstʌməkˌek/ or a sharp toothache /ˈtuθˌek/ in your stomach /ˈstʌmək/?
💡 單字對照: stomachache = 胃痛 | toothache = 牙痛 | stomach = 胃/肚子

4. [Does] your shoulder /ˈʃoldɚ/ or your throat /θrot/ hurt /hɝt/ when you catch a cold /kold/?
💡 單字對照: shoulder = 肩膀 | throat = 喉嚨 | hurt = 疼痛 | cold = 感冒

5. [Does] he have a dry cough /kɔf/, a painful earache /ˈɪrˌek/, or a sore backache /ˈbækˌek/ today?
💡 單字對照: cough = 咳嗽 | earache = 耳朵痛 | backache = 背痛

6. The tired /taɪrd/ boy does not cry /kraɪ/ or climb /klaɪm/ trees at the hospital /ˈhɑspətḷ/.
💡 單字對照: tired = 疲累的 | cry = 哭泣 | climb = 攀爬 | hospital = 醫院
