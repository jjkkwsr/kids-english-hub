Part 2: Speaking Task Reference (朗讀任務問句助動詞圈選與 KK 音標參考)

1. [Does] the doctor with a dark moustache check your neck and back?
💡 音標: doctor /ˈdɑktɚ/ (醫生) | moustache /ˈmʌstæʃ/ (八字鬍) | neck /nɛk/ (脖子) | back /bæk/ (背部)

2. [Does] the kind nurse help the patient with a bad headache and a high temperature?
💡 音標: nurse /nɝs/ (護理師) | headache /ˈhɛdˌek/ (頭痛) | temperature /ˈtɛmpɚətʃɚ/ (體溫/發燒)

3. [Do] you have a severe stomachache or a sharp toothache in your stomach?
💡 音標: stomachache /ˈstʌməkˌek/ (胃痛) | toothache /ˈtuθˌek/ (牙痛) | stomach /ˈstʌmək/ (胃/肚子)

4. [Does] your shoulder or your throat hurt when you catch a cold?
💡 音標: shoulder /ˈʃoldɚ/ (肩膀) | throat /θrot/ (喉嚨) | hurt /hɝt/ (疼痛) | cold /kold/ (感冒)

5. [Does] he have a dry cough, a painful earache, or a sore backache today?
💡 音標: cough /kɔf/ (咳嗽) | earache /ˈɪrˌek/ (耳朵痛) | backache /ˈbækˌek/ (背痛)

6. The tired boy does not cry or climb trees at the hospital.
💡 音標: tired /taɪrd/ (疲累的) | cry /kraɪ/ (哭泣) | climb /klaɪm/ (攀爬) | hospital /ˈhɑspətḷ/ (醫院)
