Part 2: Speaking Task Reference (朗讀任務進行式圈選參考)

1. Uncle Charlie [is checking] the address /əˈdrɛs/ on the map /mæp/ for people around the world /wɝld/.
💡 單字對照: address = 地址 | map = 地圖 | world = 世界

2. A guest [is taking] the elevator /ˈɛləˌvetɚ/ (or lift /lɪft/) from the first /fɝst/ floor /flɔr/ to go upstairs /ˌʌpˈstɛrz/.
💡 單字對照: elevator/lift = 電梯 | first = 第一 | floor = 樓層 | upstairs = 樓上

3. The boy [is staying] awake /əˈwek/ downstairs /ˌdaʊnˈstɛrz/ on a comfortable seat /sit/ near the road /rod/.
💡 單字對照: awake = 醒著的 | downstairs = 樓下 | seat = 座位 | road = 道路

4. She [is taking] a hot shower /ˈʃaʊɚ/ to wash /wɑʃ/ her hair and dry /draɪ/ herself with a towel /ˈtaʊəl/.
💡 單字對照: shower = 淋浴 | wash = 清洗 | dry = 弄乾 | towel = 毛巾

5. They [are swimming] in the pool /pul/ (or swimming pool /ˈswɪmɪŋ pul/) while Uncle Charlie [is pulling] a warm blanket /ˈblæŋkɪt/ and trying to call /kɔl/ us.
💡 單字對照: pool/swimming pool = 游泳池 | blanket = 毛毯 | call = 呼喚/打電話
