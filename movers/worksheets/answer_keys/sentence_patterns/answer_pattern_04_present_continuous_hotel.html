🔑 TEACHER'S ANSWER KEY (教師/家長範例解答卷)

Lesson 4 Sentence Pattern Answer Key: Uncle Charlie's Hotel

Challenge 1: Affirmative Present Continuous (示範造句解答)
1. The guest is checking the hotel address.
2. My mother is pulling a warm blanket.
Challenge 2: Negative Present Continuous (示範造句解答)
1. The children aren't swimming in the pool.
2. The tourist isn't taking a hot shower downstairs.
Challenge 3: Present Continuous Questions & Short Answers (示範造句解答)
1. Q: Is she calling from upstairs? — Ans: Yes, she is.
2. Q: Are they drying their wet towels? — Ans: No, they aren't.
Challenge 4: Location & Floor Actions (示範造句解答)
1. The driver is waiting near the road.
2. My brother is sitting on a comfortable seat upstairs.
