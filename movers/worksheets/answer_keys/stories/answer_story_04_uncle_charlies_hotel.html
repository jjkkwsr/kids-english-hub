🔑 TEACHER'S ANSWER KEY (教師解答卷)

Lesson 4 Story Answer Key: Uncle Charlie's Hotel

Story 1: A Busy Morning at the Hotel — Answer Passage
Welcome to Uncle Charlie's hotel! Guests from all over the world are arriving today. The friendly receptionist is writing down a guest's home address. Right now, Uncle Charlie is calling a waiter to help with the heavy bags. Some visitors are taking the fast elevator (or lift) to go upstairs to their guest rooms on the first floor, while others are walking downstairs to have breakfast in the lobby.
Story 2: Fun at the Hotel Swimming Pool — Answer Passage
Outside the hotel, the sun is shining! Many happy children are swimming in the big outdoor swimming pool. A boy is sitting on a comfortable seat near the water, and his mother is drying her wet hair with a soft white towel. Over by the garden, a staff member is washing the dirty floor tiles. Near the main entrance, a driver is checking a road map before steering the car onto the long open road.
Story 3: Good Night at Uncle Charlie's Hotel — Answer Passage
It is night time at the hotel. After playing all day, Tom is taking a warm shower in his room. He turns off the tap when his hair is dry. He gets into bed and pulls a soft blue blanket up to his chest. His little brother is still awake, reading a storybook on the wooden floor. Soon, everyone is fast asleep!
